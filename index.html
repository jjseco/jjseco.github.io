Project 01Environmental Assessment · GIS · Python

WA Environmental Screening Tool

I developed this tool to explore how environmental constraints can be identified more efficiently during the early assessment of a site. It brings together more than 20 Western Australian government spatial datasets covering wetlands, acid sulfate soils, groundwater, contaminated sites, heritage, sensitive receptors and other environmental considerations. The project combines environmental assessment, GIS and Python to produce an initial picture of potential constraints that may require further investigation.

GIS · Python · GeoPandas · Environmental Assessment

Project 02Contaminated Land · Environmental Assessment

NEPM Assessment Tool

I developed this interactive tool while building my understanding of contaminated land assessment in Australia. It brings together key concepts from the National Environment Protection (Assessment of Site Contamination) Measure and explores how Tier 1 assessment logic can be organised into a practical workflow. The tool is intended to support understanding and navigation of the assessment framework, not to replace site investigation or professional interpretation.

Contaminated Land · NEPM · Environmental Assessment · Interactive Tool

Project 03Remote Sensing · Environmental Change

Lake Toolibin Vegetation Change

A remote sensing project examining long-term vegetation change around Lake Toolibin in Western Australia. I used multi-temporal satellite imagery and spatial analysis to investigate how vegetation patterns changed over approximately three decades and to interpret those changes in their environmental context.

Remote Sensing · GIS · Vegetation Change · Satellite Imagery

Project 04Ecological Assessment · Field Survey

Native Small Mammal Persistence in Fragmented Landscapes, Narrikup WA

A field ecology study investigating native small mammal persistence and predator pressure across remnant vegetation patches in south-western Western Australia. The project combined camera trapping, vegetation surveys, GIS and statistical analysis in R to examine relationships between habitat condition, landscape context and species observations.

Ecological Assessment · Camera Trapping · R · ArcGIS Pro · Field Survey

About

About

My background combines biology, environmental management and environmental science. I began my career in scientific research and later moved into applied environmental work, including environmental monitoring, ecological rehabilitation, field assessment and projects with rural communities in Colombia.

After moving to Australia, I completed a Master of Environmental Science at UWA, specialising in Environmental Rehabilitation. This added an Australian context to my previous experience, particularly across environmental assessment, contaminated land, mining environmental management, rehabilitation, GIS and remote sensing.

Today, I bring those areas together in the way I approach environmental work. I am comfortable in the field, working with environmental and spatial data, and communicating technical findings. I am particularly interested in work where understanding site conditions, environmental risks and ecological processes can inform environmental management and rehabilitation.

I am based in Perth and am continuing to build my career in environmental consulting and mine-site environmental management in Western Australia.

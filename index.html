01 About

Hi, I'm Juan.

Environmental scientist with a Biology background and a completed Master in Environmental Rehabilitation from UWA. I started my career in Colombia doing contaminated land assessments, soil and groundwater sampling, and writing compliance reports. I also spent four years teaching environmental education to over 500 students, which taught me how to communicate complex ideas to very different audiences. Along the way I published a peer-reviewed paper in biochemistry from my bachelor's thesis.

I moved to Western Australia to specialise in rehabilitation. Since then I've interned at DWER, built open-source environmental tools with Python and GIS, and volunteered at Kanyana Wildlife Rehabilitation Centre, where helping recover native fauna gave me a real connection with Australian landscapes and conservation beyond the technical work.

I'm looking for opportunities in mine site rehabilitation and contaminated land remediation in WA. This is where I share the tools I build along the way.

03 Capabilities

Skills & Competencies

Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

Satellite and aerial imagery analysis, spectral indices, radiometric correction, Google Earth Engine, change detection applied to vegetation monitoring, rehabilitation tracking and land condition assessment

GIS & Spatial Analysis

QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, GeoPandas, Google Earth Engine, Folium. Applied to environmental approvals, ecological surveys, mine closure planning, rehabilitation monitoring and cartographic deliverables with QA/QC

Programming & Data

Python for data processing pipelines, automation and spatial analysis. R for statistical interpretation and data visualisation. Streamlit for web application development. Reproducible reporting and workflow automation

Field & Assessment

Site assessments, soil and groundwater sampling, validation sampling, chain-of-custody, QA/QC, site inspections, field team coordination, biological sampling and taxonomic identification

Environmental Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation planning and monitoring design, mine site environmental management, mine closure considerations, soil management, ecological assessment, measuring outcomes in disturbed landscapes

Regulatory & Compliance

WA and national frameworks: Contaminated Sites Act 2003, EP Act 1986, Mining Act 1978, NEPM. Translating regulatory standards into compliant field programs and defensible technical reporting

Technical Communication

Compliance reports, environmental management plans, briefing notes, technical reporting, stakeholder engagement across field teams, regulators and community groups
